# Continuity guide

## Character lock

Stable visible description, approximate age range, face/hair description when fictional or user-authorized reference is supplied, wardrobe, accessories, body proportions, and acting style.

## Product lock

Use supplied product facts and reference images. Preserve silhouette, scale, color, surface/texture, label, packaging, and variant. Never invent hidden ingredients, internal construction, or brand packaging.

## Scene lock

Preserve location, time of day, light direction, background props, camera grammar, and color treatment. Record the exact hand/object position and movement at each scene boundary.
