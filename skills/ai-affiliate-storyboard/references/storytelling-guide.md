# Three-scene storytelling guide

- Scene 1 — Discovery / Hook: establish a relatable context, introduce the product, and create curiosity.
- Scene 2 — Product Experience: demonstrate one or more confirmed differentiators through credible actions.
- Scene 3 — Payoff / Recommendation: show a natural reaction or practical outcome, reinforce the value, and close with an optional CTA.

Maintain a single story arc. Avoid repeating the same benefit in every scene. The emotional payoff is a narrative possibility, not a guaranteed product result.
