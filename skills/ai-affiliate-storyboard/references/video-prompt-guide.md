# Video prompt guide

Default: 10 seconds per scene, vertical 9:16, three scenes total. Use a time-coded beat list whose durations sum to the scene duration. Include natural movement, camera behavior, audio/narration, and visual constraints. Explicitly request a continuous moving scene, not a static storyboard or animated contact sheet. Scene 2 begins from Scene 1's final action/pose; Scene 3 begins from Scene 2's final action/pose.
